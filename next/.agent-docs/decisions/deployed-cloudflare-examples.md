- (agent) `examples/cloudflare/` covers what @trpc12/trpc ships for Cloudflare: a Worker routing to a Durable Object per room, hibernating WebSockets with `experimental_hibernatingPublisher`, and a durable streamed mutation (`digest`) whose results and frames live in the object's storage (`src/storage.ts`: Effect's `KeyValueStore` on `DurableObjectStorage`, under `Persistence.layerBackingKvs`).
