# Docs pages nest under a parent page, and sections open with an overview (human asked)

- Human, 2026-10-18: "I feel like I'm missing overview pages or multiple levels in navigation, for instance there's maaaany built links but they don't have specific navigation".
- (agent) A page names its parent with `parent: <slug>` in its frontmatter, a page of the same section. The build fails on a parent that isn't a page, is in another section, or forms a cycle (`readingOrder` in `www/build/content.ts`). Children follow their parent in reading order (previous and next links, `llms.txt`), sorted by `order`, so a nested page's `order` counts among its siblings.
- (agent) The sidebar is a tree: a parent has a chevron that folds its children, and the branch holding the current page starts open (also in the prerendered HTML). A parent page shows its ancestors in the breadcrumb and ends with "Pages under …" cards of its children.
- (agent) Every built-in link has a page under Client > Links (`docs/client/links/`: `httpLink`, `wsLink`, `splitLink`, `switchLink`, `retryLink`, `dedupeLink`, `loggerLink`, `localLink`), with `validationLink` and Custom links nested there too. `client/links.md` is the overview: the chain, and a table of the terminating links and the links in the middle. Publisher backends nest under Publisher.
- (agent) Server, Middleware, Integrations and OpenAPI open with an overview page (`label: Overview`, `order: 0`), as Client, Subscriptions and Adapters already did. Middleware's overview is the list of built-in middleware and the layer each needs, which `server/middleware.md` used to carry.
- (agent) Links whose v12 page is small, or which live elsewhere (`experimental_socketLink`, `experimental_messagePortLink`, `openApiLink`, `experimental_durableLink`), stay a row of the Links tables pointing at the page that covers them, rather than a page each.
- (agent) The v11 copy nests the same way, as trpc.io's sidebar does ([`v11-docs-copy.md`](./v11-docs-copy.md)).
- Why: one long Links page made each link hard to find and link to; a tree keeps the sidebar short (a section shows its overview pages until one is opened) while every topic has its own URL, title and social card.
- Lost: a flat sidebar where every page is one click away; a section can't hold a nested group without a parent page (no label-only folders).
- Lives in: `next/www/build/content.ts` (`Frontmatter.parent`, `readingOrder`, `llmsTxt`), `next/www/src/content/docs.ts` (`ancestors`, `childrenOf`), `next/www/src/components/docs-nav.tsx`, `next/www/src/components/doc-page.tsx`, `parent:` in `next/docs/**/*.md`, `next/docs/client/links/`.
