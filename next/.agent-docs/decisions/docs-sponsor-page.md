- (agent) `/sponsor` is a page of its own: a hero with the sponsor count, a "We need the money for tokens" section (v12 is written mostly by agents, with a terminal running out of credits), the four monthly tiers as cards ($50 Indie, Bronze, Silver and Gold, the last two badged Partner), where partners show up (the current partners and a "Your logo here" slot), the sponsor cloud, and questions (trpc or KATT, when a logo shows up, invoicing, what became of the Pro plan).
