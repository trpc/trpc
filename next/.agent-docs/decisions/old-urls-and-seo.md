# trpc.io's old URLs redirect here, and the site's SEO is at least trpc.io's (human asked)

- Human, 2026-10-18: "fix all seo at least on par with old www and make sure all old routes in the vercel json still redirects correctly".
- (agent) Every URL trpc.io answers redirects once, to where trpc.io sent it, as this site serves it. That covers the redirects in its `vercel.json` (copied verbatim to `www/trpc-io-redirects.json` by `old-docs:copy`, and checked by `old-docs:check`), its pages (`/docs/<v11 slug>`, `/docs/v10/…`, `/docs/v9/…`, `/blog/…`) and its copied images (`/img/…`). `build/old-routes.ts` follows trpc.io's redirect chain (keeping the `#hash` a hop adds) and maps where it ends: the copied page of its version (v11's at the same `/docs/…` path, `/v10/docs/…`, `/v9/docs/…`), a post, or a URL off the site (`/discord`). A redirect with params (`/docs/reactjs/:path`, `/docs/v11/:path*`) answers every URL whose destination is a page here. A URL this site already serves keeps its page, so trpc.io's v11 pages need no redirect.
- (agent) A redirect is permanent (308) when every hop of trpc.io's is. Until 2026-10-18 v12 had `/docs` and a v11 URL went to `/v11/docs/…`, temporarily (307), unless v12 had a page at its slug; since v11 has `/docs` ([`docs-v11-by-default.md`](./docs-v11-by-default.md)), every v11 URL lands where trpc.io sends it, and `/v11/docs/*` redirects to `/docs/*`.
- (agent) Three of trpc.io's redirects point at pages that never existed, there too (`/docs/server/api-handler`, `/docs/aborting-procedures`, `/docs/extra`); `fixedRedirects` sends them where they meant to go. Pages trpc.io has that no sidebar lists (`/blog/archive`, `/blog/authors`, the home page's `landing-intro` steps, a Twoslash test page) go to `/blog` or `/` (`retiredPages`). What only trpc.io has (the API reference under `/docs/typedoc`, `/media`, `/pricing`, `/search`, its other static files) redirects there (307), while this site isn't trpc.io.
- (agent) The build checks it: `build/vercel-output.ts` runs the routes it writes the way Vercel does (`respond`, `land`), and fails when an old URL doesn't land on a file in one redirect, when a redirect's `#hash` is a heading the page doesn't have, or when a route hides a file. Matching redirects share a route (`/docs/v10(/a|/b)` to `/v10/docs$1`), so 405 redirects take 88 routes. On 2026-10-18 all 703 URLs in trpc.io's sitemap and `vercel.json` landed on a page here, or on trpc.io for the API reference.
- (agent) What trpc.io's pages had in their `<head>` that this site's didn't is added: `og:locale`, `hreflang` alternates (`en`, `x-default`), `article:published_time` and `article:author` on posts, `favicon.ico` (16, 32 and 48 px, rendered from `favicon.svg` with resvg, with `apple-touch-icon.png`), and the blog's RSS and Atom feeds at trpc.io's `/blog/rss.xml` and `/blog/atom.xml`, linked from every page. Beyond trpc.io: `twitter:site`, `og:image:type`, `article:modified_time` and `article:section` on v12 pages, the social card as each article's JSON-LD `image`, and a `WebSite` node on the home page. It already had titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, per-page social cards, `robots.txt`, a sitemap and breadcrumbs.
- Why: links and search results to trpc.io keep working wherever this site is served, and the build catches the day one stops.
- Lost: trpc.io's `meta keywords` (no page had any), its OpenSearch description (the search here is a dialog with no URL), the feeds' full post HTML (entries carry the summary), and Google Analytics, which isn't SEO.
- (agent) Until it's trpc.io, the site is `noindex` everywhere ([`site-noindex-until-trpc-io.md`](./site-noindex-until-trpc-io.md)); the parity above is what search engines get from the cutover on.
- Open: when this site becomes trpc.io, the copies' canonical links (`trpcPageUrl`) and the `onTrpcIo` redirects need rethinking (`onTrpcIoRoutes` already drops itself on that domain). Also open: whether v11 URLs should go to their v12 counterparts (the `v11` frontmatter) once v12 is stable.
