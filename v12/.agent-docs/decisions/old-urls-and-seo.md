# trpc.io's old URLs redirect here, and the site's SEO is at least trpc.io's (human asked)

- Human, 2026-10-18: "fix all seo at least on par with old www and make sure all old routes in the vercel json still redirects correctly".
- (agent) Since 2026-11-01 the site is #7771's, which serves trpc.io's pages at trpc.io's own URLs (`/docs/<v11 slug>`, `/docs/v10/…`, `/docs/v9/…`, `/blog/…`) and keeps its redirects in `www/vercel.json`, `main`'s, in `main`'s order, which merges from `main` keep current. The Build Output API doesn't read `vercel.json`'s `redirects`, so `build/old-routes.ts` turns each into a route, in order, as Vercel would, and `build/vercel-output.ts` fails the build on one that lands nowhere. Until then, v12's site copied the redirects into `trpc-io-redirects.json` and mapped them onto its own URLs (`/v10/docs/…`); [`one-workspace.md`](./one-workspace.md) has the move.
- (agent) `/v11/docs/*`, v12's old address for the v11 pages, redirects to `/docs/*` (308), since v11 has `/docs` ([`docs-v11-by-default.md`](./docs-v11-by-default.md)).
- (agent) The build checks it: `build/legacy-urls-check.ts` runs at its end and sends each URL of trpc.io's sitemap (`build/trpc-io-urls.txt`, 583) and each redirect source in `vercel.json` without params through the routes the build wrote, failing on one that doesn't land on a page or off the site; `--base <url>` checks a deploy over HTTP.
- (agent) What trpc.io's pages had in their `<head>` that this site's didn't is added: `og:locale`, `hreflang` alternates (`en`, `x-default`), `article:published_time` and `article:author` on posts, `favicon.ico` (16, 32 and 48 px, rendered from `favicon.svg` with resvg, with `apple-touch-icon.png`), and the blog's RSS and Atom feeds at trpc.io's `/blog/rss.xml` and `/blog/atom.xml`, linked from every page. Beyond trpc.io: `twitter:site`, `og:image:type`, `article:modified_time` and `article:section` on v12 pages, the social card as each article's JSON-LD `image`, and a `WebSite` node on the home page. It already had titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, per-page social cards, `robots.txt`, a sitemap and breadcrumbs.
- Why: links and search results to trpc.io keep working wherever this site is served, and the build catches the day one stops.
- Lost: trpc.io's `meta keywords` (no page had any), its OpenSearch description until [`trpc-io-cutover-and-hidden-v12.md`](./trpc-io-cutover-and-hidden-v12.md) brought it back with `/search`, the feeds' full post HTML (entries carry the summary), and Google Analytics, which isn't SEO.
- (agent) Until it's trpc.io, the site is `noindex` everywhere ([`site-noindex-until-trpc-io.md`](./site-noindex-until-trpc-io.md)); the parity above is what search engines get from the cutover on.
- Open: when this site becomes trpc.io, the copies' canonical links and the `onTrpcIo` redirects: settled in [`trpc-io-cutover-and-hidden-v12.md`](./trpc-io-cutover-and-hidden-v12.md). Also open: whether v11 URLs should go to their v12 counterparts (the `v11` frontmatter) once v12 is stable.
- Lives in: `www/vercel.json` (`redirects`), `www/build/old-routes.ts`, `www/build/vercel-output.ts`, `www/build/legacy-urls-check.ts`, `www/build/trpc-io-urls.txt`, `www/build/feeds.ts`, `www/build/icons.ts`, `www/src/seo.ts`, `www/src/routes/__root.tsx`, `www/vite.config.ts`.
